### Chapter 9: Post-market Monitoring, Information Sharing and Market Surveillance

#### Section 5: Supervision, investigation, enforcement and monitoring in respect of providers of general-purpose AI models

##### Article 94: Procedural rights of economic operators of the general-purpose AI model

###### (1)

Art. 18 of Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 shall apply mutatis mutandis to the providers of the general-purpose AI model, without prejudice to more specific procedural rights provided for in this Regulation.
